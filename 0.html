02 · Classification
02 · Data Classification
Legal asks if we're storing PII. I honestly don't know where. — security lead

We see what's inside your data.

DataProtect scans every table, file, and bucket and classifies sensitive data by category — then governs it with the compliance policies you operate under. Together they tell you what needs the strongest protection.

ReplacesManual compliance audits
  • Categories describe what the data is — Personal data, Health data, Card data, Financial, Credentials.
  • Policies describe how it must be handled — Card-data rules (PCI DSS v4), Health rules (HIPAA), EU privacy (GDPR), India privacy (DPDP).
  • Criticality = Category × Policy. A Personal-data column under Health rules is far more critical than the same column outside it.
  • Scan scope is explicit. Choose source-side or snapshot-side — you always know where classification ran.
