02 · Pre-checks · audit-ready

Know it'll work —
before you run it.

Pre-checks run inline as you build the recovery — one for the workload, one for the recovery point, one for the target. Expired agent, unreachable target, broken snapshot — all surface at 3 PM, not at 3 AM during the actual recovery.

  • Stepwise, not at the end. Each step runs its own pre-check before you move to the next.
  • No mid-recovery surprises. What would have failed at 3 AM flags at 3 PM.
  • Readiness report. Every pre-check is timestamped — printable evidence of "tested recoverable on Mon, Wed, Fri."

03 · Recovery isn't one thing

Try it safely —
before you trust it.

Recovery comes in flavors: file, application, instance, infrastructure, cleanroom — into the same source, a different source, a different cloud, or a virtually-gapped sandbox. Pick the right shape for the situation.

  • Test recovery. Same path, tiny scope, free learning.
  • Cleanroom recovery. For infected snapshots — recover into an isolated environment first.
  • Cross-cloud recovery. Bring up an AWS workload on Azure when AWS is down.
